Matvey the Bear is handcrafted using a vintage Soviet pattern from the 1950s. Standing at a very convenient size of 13 cm, he can easily become your pocket companion or happily sit on a shelf. The bear is made of German viscose, features glass eyes, and is stuffed with cedar sawdust and metal granulate, giving him a pleasant, solid weight. The firm stuffing ensures the toy holds its shape perfectly, and he is beautifully accessorized with authentic Soviet lace and ribbon.
