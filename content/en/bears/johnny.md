Baby Johnny in the traditional Teddy technique.
When creating this monkey, I was inspired by rare antique toys from Germany in the 1930s. I wanted to convey this characteristic graphic contrast: a light felt face, fluffy white mohair on the head (Germany, very soft), and a dark, textured body (also mohair, Germany). I found the house 🏡
