The name Harlequin reflects not so much his outfit as his character. Thanks to the combination of two fur colors, the teddy bear has a different mood depending on the angle. From one angle, it seems childlike and bright, while from another, it appears serious, contemplative, and lost in thought. This is the image of an artist in silence.

Height: 18 cm. Sitting: 14 cm. Mohair, Germany. Feet and palms — wool felt (Germany), reinforced. Sewn-on glass eyes (Germany). Head and paws are movable on 5 pins. Stuffing is dense, the body is slightly softer. Cedar sawdust, cedar wood wool, metal and glass granulate.
The cap and the frill collar are removable. (Batiste, leather). A pendant on a paw with a glass bead (Czech Republic).
The nose is embroidered, with light toning using oil paints.
